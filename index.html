Sheet 03 — Process

The Milto Process

Precision engineering from initial study through final handover — a proven methodology delivering reliable water solutions across Ethiopia.

01

Study & Investigation

Comprehensive data collection, hydrogeological surveys, and technical analysis to understand site conditions and water resource potential.

02

Design & Planning

Engineered solutions with detailed specifications, drawings, and cost estimates tailored to project requirements and regulatory standards.

03

Construction Supervision

On-site oversight, quality control, and contract administration ensuring implementation meets design specifications and safety standards.

04

Training & Handover

Knowledge transfer, operational training, and systematic handover ensuring long-term sustainability and client self-sufficiency.

Sheet 06 — Who We Are

Mission, Vision, Values

Milto Engineering PLC is a grade-one water resources and related-fields consulting firm, established in August 2021 by experienced professionals.

Mission

To provide professional service in study, design, contract administration and construction supervision of projects to the highest standards of quality, within the agreed timeframe and at reasonable prices — through long-term partnership to the satisfaction of clients, employees, the public and the environment at large.

Vision

Milto Engineering PLC aspires to be a center of excellence in providing technical consulting services in Ethiopia.

Values
  • Dedication to the welfare of the people
  • Commitment to clients' satisfaction
  • Transparency, accountability, fairness
  • Cost consciousness
  • Teamwork spirit and innovation
