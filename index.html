Sheet 02 — Method

We read the ground before we design for it.

Every water supply or drilling project starts below the surface. Our hydrogeological cross-sections map aquifer type, groundwater flow direction and static water level along the full profile — the evidence base for every recommendation we make.

Borehole geophysical logging, camera investigations and topographic survey feed directly into this picture before a single well is sited.
